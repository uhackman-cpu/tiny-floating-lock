Privacy Policy — Tiny Floating Lock Last updated: October 27, 2026 Tiny Floating Lock is an Android utility published by Dev Kashyap. It provides a floating button for user-triggered screen locking and quick controls for the button's appearance and media volume. Information collection and sharing The current version is designed not to collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, user accounts, or app network requests, and it does not request the Android Internet permission. Preferences such as button position, size, opacity, and gesture settings are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer or third parties. Accessibility service The app uses Android's Accessibility Service so the floating button can perform actions you initiate, including locking the screen, opening Android's Power Menu when double-tapped, and showing quick controls when held. You must enable the service manually in Android Settings after the in-app disclosure and consent step. The service is configured not to retrieve other apps' window content. It does not inspect screen text, messages, passwords, typed text, or other apps' content, does not record the screen, and does not transmit accessibility data. The service is used for app functionality, not advertising, analytics, profiling, or remote monitoring. You can disable it at any time in Android Accessibility settings. Some system menus and labels vary by Android version and device manufacturer. Information stored on your device The app stores local preferences such as the floating button's position, size, opacity, and volume-gesture setting in app storage on your device. These preferences remain on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Permissions and control Accessibility access is required for the core lock action and floating controls. You can disable the service at any time in Android Accessibility settings and uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Android system settings govern device-lock credentials and biometric authentication; this app does not collect them. Advertising and future changes This policy describes the current version, which does not contain ads. If advertising, analytics, or other data-processing features are added in a future version, this policy and the app's Google Play disclosures will be updated before that version is released. Children's privacy Tiny Floating Lock is a general-purpose device utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The current version does not collect or transmit personal information. Data retention and deletion The app is designed not to send user preferences or personal information to the developer. There is no developer-hosted user account or profile to delete. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove locally stored preferences, subject to Android's normal device behavior. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated when the app's features or legal requirements change.
