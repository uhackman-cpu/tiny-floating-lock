Tiny Floating Lock — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Tiny Floating Lock is an Android utility that provides a movable floating button for user-triggered screen locking and related device controls. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

The current version of Tiny Floating Lock does not require an account and is designed not to collect, sell, or share personal information. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not request the Android Internet permission. App preferences such as button position, size, opacity, and gesture settings are stored locally on your device.

Accessibility Service

The app offers an optional Android Accessibility Service to perform actions you explicitly initiate, including locking the screen, opening the Android Power Menu, and supporting the floating controls. You must enable the service yourself in Android Settings after the app explains its purpose and asks for your consent.

The service is not used to collect or transmit screen text, messages, passwords, or content from other apps. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or remote monitoring. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings. Some device actions and menus can vary by Android version or manufacturer.

Sharing and third parties

The current version does not send app preference data to us or to third parties. No ad network or analytics provider is integrated into the current release. If a future release changes data practices or adds third-party services, this policy will be updated before that release is distributed.

Children

Tiny Floating Lock does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data retention and deletion

Because the current version is designed not to transmit personal information to the developer, we do not maintain a server-side user profile or app-data account to delete. You can clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app preferences, subject to Android's normal device behavior.

Security

Use the Accessibility Service only if you understand and agree with the actions described in the app. Keep Android and the app updated, and disable the service whenever you no longer need it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
