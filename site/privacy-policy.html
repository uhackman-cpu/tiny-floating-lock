Privacy Policy — Tiny Floating Lock

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Tiny Floating Lock is an Android utility published by Dev Kashyap. It provides a floating button for locking the device screen and quick controls for the button appearance and media volume.

Information collection and sharing

The current version of Tiny Floating Lock does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, accounts, or network requests, and it does not send personal information to the developer or third parties. The app does not request the Android Internet permission.

Accessibility service

The app asks the user to enable its Accessibility Service so that the floating lock button can use Android's screen-lock and power-dialog actions and display its accessibility overlay. When the power menu is opened, the service may briefly inspect accessibility labels exposed by Android's system UI to look for a Lockdown option. This check runs locally on the device; the app does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit the contents of other apps' screens, messages, passwords, or user-entered text. The service can be disabled in Android Settings.

Information stored on your device

The app stores local preferences such as the floating button's position, size, opacity, and volume-gesture setting on your device. These preferences are not sent to the developer.

Permissions and control

Accessibility access is required for the core lock action and floating controls. You can disable the service at any time in Android Accessibility settings and uninstall the app to remove it. Android system settings govern device lock credentials and biometric authentication; this app does not collect them.

Advertising and future changes

This policy describes the current version, which does not contain ads. If advertising, analytics, or other data-processing features are added in a future version, this policy and the app's Google Play disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose device utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. The current version does not collect or transmit personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page's URL.
